Subscribe to the **STATE OF THE <span style="color:#1c75bc;">ARG</span>** mailing list to receive announcements and reminders about upcoming seminars.

[Subscribe to the mailing list](https://lists.uoregon.edu/mailman/listinfo/state_of_the_arg)

## How to subscribe

After submitting your email address on the subscription page, you will receive a confirmation email from the mailing list. **You must click the confirmation link in that email to complete your subscription and become a member of the mailing list.** If you do not see the message, please check your spam or junk folder. Your subscription is not active until you have completed this confirmation step.
